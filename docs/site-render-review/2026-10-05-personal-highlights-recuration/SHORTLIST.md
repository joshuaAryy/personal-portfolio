## Reference-page storytelling observations

Inspected [Shiv Arora's About page](https://shiv-arora.netlify.app/about.html) in installed Chrome 153 at a 1440-pixel viewport. Scrolled the page to trigger lazy-loaded media and reviewed its full-page render and text.

- It opens with a first-person note that tells visitors why these photos belong there: they are the parts of a life that do not fit neatly into case studies.
- It organizes the page with loose, human chapter titles such as "Where it started," "Building, breaking, and figuring things out," and "Small moments." The titles create an arc without making the page feel like a resume taxonomy.
- Captions add a specific reason or angle for the image, rather than repeating its visible subject. They range from factual context to understated humor. The car-diagnostic pair is a small sequence: attempt, then outcome.
- Image scale and placement vary. Candid people, technical work, food, travel, and odd moments share one personal voice rather than being separated into strict categories.
- A candid photo with a friend is used as the opening image, with one direct sentence of context.

Use these storytelling moves as principles only. No styling or layout from that page is prescribed here.
