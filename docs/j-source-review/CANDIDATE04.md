## Direction

Open Crest restores an orbital gesture without enclosing the J. A tapered gold crescent rises on the left and overlaps into the hook. Its top ends before the crown; there is no rim across the top or around the right. The letter carries the broad sweeping crown, thick warm-metal shaft, and lifted hook associated with the archive/v8 family. Original gold planes, bevels, wear cuts, and extrusion provide the forged material; restrained cyan filaments occupy the open counter rather than forming Candidate 02's external S stroke or Candidate 03's inset seam.

The new whole-mark silhouette and spatial cyan treatment distinguish this direction from Candidates 01–03. It uses League-inspired gold/cyan material cues with original paths; it does not import Riot's logo geometry.

## Visual observations and tradeoffs

- **Presentation:** the J remains dominant and the open left crescent provides an asymmetric crest signature. Cyan has more atmosphere than Candidate 03, but still looks deliberately drawn. The crescent/hook overlap is visually busy; its bevels show a junction rather than one seamless forged surface.
- **54px:** the J and crescent remain distinguishable. Cyan contracts into a small luminous gesture; fine wear cuts disappear.
- **32px:** the crown and hook carry the letter. The crescent is visible, but cyan and recess detail compress. This is busier than Candidate 03's freestanding mark.
- **16px:** the complete crest is crowded and loses material separation. Use the separate solid ring-free J for this size; the full mark is evaluation evidence, not a recommended 16px production asset.

The principal review tradeoff is atmosphere and whole-mark signature versus simplicity. Candidate 04 avoids a complete badge/rim, but its left crescent still carries a partial crest association. Whether that association improves the portfolio's first impression is an owner decision. These are specialist visual observations, not acceptance or production readiness claims.
