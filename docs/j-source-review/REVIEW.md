## Current findings

The archive has the stronger finished first read. The reconstruction studies flatten the upper structure and make the J too squat and the hook too blunt. Return to the archive’s overall vertical proportion, distinctive crown/upper serif, and lifted hook. Judge the letter before material effects, then assess whether the orbit feels joined to the identity rather than drawn as a badge around it.

Pass31 tests a taller archive-like proportion with a slimmer shaft, a lifted tapered terminal, and one open orbit sweep connecting the crown and hook. Pass32 tests a tighter crown and a higher hook terminal. Both remain reversible study candidates; neither is an approved direction or production mark.

Pass33 tests the archive-derived silhouette without a medallion and a single orbit that meets the crown and hook. Pass34 confirms the archive image can be cropped to the J, but the source is low resolution and contains color spill inside translucent areas; it is not a production asset. Pass35 rebuilds the same archive contour as a crisp vector, restores the original crown bevel and hook contour, and tests one asymmetric orbit. Pass36 compares that J at the same 300px size with the orbit joined into the hook and with no orbit; it also shows the joined version at 300/150/54/32px and a ring-free 16px glyph. The letterform-only option has the cleanest immediate J read. The joined orbit adds identity and now tucks under the hook, but still risks a crescent/bracket read. Both remain reversible, unapproved studies; the archive target remains the standard.

The `3089:2` board directly compares the archive, current reconstruction, Pass30, both upper-serif studies, and Pass31/32. Pass36 `3123:2` compares the joined-orbit and letterform-only versions at equal 300px size, then proves the joined orbit at 300/150/54/32px and the ring-free monochrome J at 16px. These scale proofs are review aids, not evidence of final acceptance.
