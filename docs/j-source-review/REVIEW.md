## Current findings

The archive remains the quality target and strongest finished first read. Pass54 makes the reconstructed shaft more substantial; Pass55's +6% height is closer to the archive's vertical fill than +9.5%. Pass56 improves the standalone hook over its Pass43 seed, but remains too wide overall. Pass57 carries the lifted terminal to Pass35's narrower proportions; Pass58's 0.5px/0.8px tip change is too subtle to resolve the clipped-barb read. Pass59's B improves crown/weight; Pass60 C was the clearest prior orbit-free J. Pass61/62 orbit attempts remain detached or crowd the counter. Pass63 shows A tracks the archive shoulder but is too light, B best holds the upright, and C's lifted tip usefully opens the counter but remains soft. Pass65 A retains the archive-like left slope but breaks at the right shoulder; Pass65 B returns smoothly but is too compact. Pass66 blends A's slope with B's continuous shoulder and is the strongest J-only crown so far. Pass67 adds modest lower-edge weight to the left flare without a lump or heavy foot; the J reads at 468/54/32px, with the separate ring-free 16px glyph unchanged. This moves the crown somewhat closer to the archive, but the complete archive mark remains more distinctive because its crown, shaft, hook, and energy are integrated. Pass68 tests an interrupted ellipse and one outer-left sweep around fixed Pass67. A still reads as a badge; B reduces containment but remains a detached C-arc. The archive is more integrated because its energy occupies the negative bowl. Next pull B inward through the open bowl behind the J, with the letter visibly occluding it. Pass68 is a useful direction study, not an approved mark. Do not promote any editable candidate.
