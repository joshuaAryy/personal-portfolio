## Current findings

The archive remains the quality target and strongest finished first read. Pass54 makes the reconstructed shaft more substantial; Pass55's +6% height is closer to archive fill than +9.5%. Pass56 improves the standalone hook over its Pass43 seed, but remains too wide. Pass57 improves vertical read; Pass58's small tip change does not resolve the clipped-barb read. Pass59's B improves crown/weight; Pass60 C was the clearest prior orbit-free J. Pass61/62 arcs remain detached or crowd the counter. Pass63 B best holds the upright; A is too light and C's lifted hook remains soft. Pass65 A has the closer left slope but breaks at the shoulder; B is smooth but compact. Pass66 blends slope and shoulder continuity; Pass67 adds modest lower-edge weight. Pass71 now has the strongest local crown/standalone silhouette: its deeper left-serif underside flows continuously into the shoulder without a bulb or notch, and the J reads at 468/54/32px. The separate 16px glyph remains ring-free. The archive remains stronger as a complete identity, chiefly through its hook terminal and richer orbit/energy integration. Pass68 A reads as a badge; B as a detached C-arc. Pass69 A reads as a shallow underline; B's long return is badge-like and crowds the counter. Pass70 is the clearest orbit integration direction: its rising interior sweep joins the hook and hides beneath the shoulder while keeping the bowl open; J readability holds at 54/32px. Pass71 preserves that sweep. Independent critique finds Pass71's serif weight improves the macro read; the difference compresses at 32px, so preserve a distinct ring-free 16px glyph. Neither Pass70 nor Pass71 is owner-approved. Pass72 should freeze Pass71's crown and Pass70 sweep, then test one intermediate rising hook taper between Pass64 E and F while preserving the open counter. Do not promote any editable candidate.
