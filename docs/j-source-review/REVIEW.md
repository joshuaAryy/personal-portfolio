The archive remains the quality target and strongest finished first read. Pass46 `3167:2` directly compares the archive/current/upper-serif studies and identifies Pass35 as a promising integrated vector base. Pass47 `3171:2` clarified crown/orbit entry; Pass48 `3174:2` pairs it with a stronger hook return. Pass49 `3182:2` tests a narrower, rising hook terminal. Pass50 `3191:2` brings the ring-free Pass43 letterform into the same 468px field as archive, Pass35, Pass49 D, and Pass48 C. The Pass49 terminal reads more directional but more angular; Pass50 makes the wider ring-free silhouette easier to judge against the integrated forms. A scoped macro review found Pass35 the strongest editable starting point among these studies: Pass43’s crown and bowl are too wide for the archive’s tall, narrow read; Pass49 D feels like a clipped bevel; Pass48 C remains the most developed Pass35 branch. Pass51 `3201:2` applies one curved rising taper to Pass35’s outer hook, keeping its bowl/open counter, crown, shaft, and orbit fixed. At 468px the hook has a clearer lifted taper than Pass35 without the diagonal cut of Pass49 D; the 54/32px proofs retain recognition, and the editable 16px proofs isolate the J without its orbit. This is a focused unapproved candidate, not a canonical mark. The archive still leads in finished identity. Compare silhouette, crown, vertical proportion, hook counter, orbit integration, and first read before choosing a base.
