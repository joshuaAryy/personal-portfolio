## Current findings

The archive has the stronger finished first read. The reconstruction studies flatten the upper structure and make the J too squat and the hook too blunt. Return to the archive’s overall vertical proportion, distinctive crown/upper serif, and lifted hook. Judge the letter before material effects, then assess whether the orbit feels joined to the identity rather than drawn as a badge around it.

Pass31 tests a taller archive-like proportion with a slimmer shaft, a lifted tapered terminal, and one open orbit sweep connecting the crown and hook. Pass32 tests a tighter crown and a higher hook terminal. Both remain reversible study candidates; neither is an approved direction or production mark.

Pass33 tests the archive-derived silhouette without a medallion and a single orbit that meets the crown and hook. Pass34 confirms the archive image can be cropped to the J, but the source is low resolution and contains color spill inside translucent areas; it is not a production asset. Pass35 rebuilds the same archive contour as a crisp vector, restores the original crown bevel and hook contour, and tests one asymmetric orbit concealed beneath the crown and into the hook. Pass35 is the strongest current review candidate because it keeps the archive’s macro identity while removing the closed medallion. Its orbit can still read as a bracket and has not received owner review. The archive target remains the standard; Pass33/34/35 are reversible, unapproved studies.

The `3089:2` board directly compares the archive, current reconstruction, Pass30, both upper-serif studies, and Pass31/32. Pass35 `3111:2` shows its 300/150/54/32px mark proofs and a ring-free monochrome 16px J. These scale proofs are a review aid, not evidence of final acceptance.
