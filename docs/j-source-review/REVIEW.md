## Current findings

The archive remains the quality target and strongest finished first read. Pass54 makes the reconstructed shaft more substantial; Pass55's +6% height is closer to the archive's vertical fill than +9.5%. Pass56 improves the standalone hook over its Pass43 seed, but remains too wide overall. Pass57 carries the lifted terminal to Pass35's narrower proportions; Pass58's 0.5px/0.8px tip change is too subtle to resolve the clipped-barb read. Pass59 tests two broader local hypotheses against the archive/current/serif/Pass35 set: B's sloped crown and heavier shaft improve the upper silhouette and vertical read, while A's longer rising hook reads as a claw/spur. Both read as J at 54/32px, but their open orbit reads as a separate C-shaped bracket; the ring-free 16px glyph remains appropriate. The archive still leads. Next combine B's crown/weight with a shorter, smoothly curved version of A's rising hook, preserving Pass35's bowl and counter, then review the standalone letterform before orbit integration. Do not promote any editable candidate.

Pass31 tests a taller archive-like proportion with a slimmer shaft, a lifted tapered terminal, and one open orbit sweep connecting the crown and hook.
