## Current findings

The archive remains the stronger finished first read. Pass41’s wider archive-derived silhouette carries more visual weight than its source-width version; the shorter option compresses the letter too much. Pass42/43 compare the wider J with no orbit, an open arc, and a return sweep that disappears toward the shaft. The no-orbit option currently reads most clearly as a J. The open arc still reads like a bracket, while the longer sweep leaves a loose lower-right tail. These are exploratory macro comparisons, not approvals.

Pass31 tests a taller archive-like proportion with a slimmer shaft, a lifted tapered terminal, and one open orbit sweep connecting the crown and hook. Pass32 tests a tighter crown and a higher hook terminal. Both remain reversible study candidates; neither is an approved direction or production mark.

Pass33–36 rebuilt and compared the archive contour, then tested orbit integration. Pass37’s interrupted arc did not resolve the bracket read. Pass38’s longer tail was rejected and hidden; Pass39’s merge under the hook was too subtle to change the overall read. Pass40 was an inconclusive width calibration and is hidden. Pass41 then compared the baseline, +20% width, and +12% width/+8% shorter proportions. Pass42/43 carry the +20% version into orbit comparisons. The archive remains the standard and fallback; no vector candidate is canonical.
