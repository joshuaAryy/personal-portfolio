## Direction and comparison

Rift / Forged treats the letter as three gold sections separated by two diagonal cyan breaks. It retains v8's editable textured metal, broad crown, bevels and rising hook, while removing its ring and portal. A small cyan release extends from the upper break into the open counter. This is a structural fracture treatment rather than C02's external S flow, C03's simplified faceted body/inset seam, or C04's open crescent.

The whole mark uses a cloned v8 J as its material base; its continuous outer contour was not redrawn. Original dark apertures and cyan fracture edges change the segmented silhouette. The dark cuts are editable vector overlays matched to the presentation background, not boolean holes or a transparent production asset. No code or production export was made.

Compared with the archive/v8, the atmosphere is much more restrained and the letter less continuous. Compared with C01, no closed rim surrounds it. Compared with C02, the cyan belongs to the fractured shaft rather than a second expressive counter stroke. Compared with C03/C04, the gold retains v8's richer texture, but the new fractures introduce a different readability compromise. This is a serious directional review candidate, not a claim of improvement or owner preference.

## Native-size observations

- **468px:** the texture and bevels survive. The two cyan breaks create a distinct segmented signature, but the upper section can read as a detached cap and the middle as a separate gold plate. The treatment is more architectural than atmospheric.
- **54px:** the J is recognizable and both breaks are visible. Cyan is a narrow accent; the upper release mostly disappears.
- **32px:** the gaps compress and weaken the continuous shaft read. The crown and hook supply recognition. Cyan loses its material interaction.
- **16px:** the full mark loses texture and fracture detail and is less reliable than the supporting solid J. The separate unfractured glyph is clearer.

The separate ring-free supporting glyphs retain the established C03 optical construction, copied through C04. They deliberately omit the fracture motif; this preserves small-size legibility but creates a stronger visual difference between large and small identity variants.
