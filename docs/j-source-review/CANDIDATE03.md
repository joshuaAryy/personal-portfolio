## Direction and tradeoffs

Candidate 03 is a newly drawn silhouette, rather than a v8 material clone. It preserves the broad sweeping crown, rising hook, warm gold, and cold cyan. The slanted wedge stem, broader open counter, faceted metal planes, and cyan seam inset into the inner bevel distinguish it from Candidate 02's external flow. There is no surrounding badge, rim, or portal.

The material is more graphic and less atmospheric than the archive and v8. Presentation scale shows the facets and seam clearly. At 54px the silhouette and bevel separate, while cyan is subtle. At 32px the facets simplify and the crown/hook carry the J. The 16px whole mark loses the bevel and cyan; the separate solid optical glyph is clearer. These are visual observations, not owner acceptance or a production readiness claim.

## Mingo comparison — 2026-10-05

**Decision:** Candidate 03 is a materially distinct, editable owner-review candidate. It is not approved, and it does not replace the archive in production. Sonnet v8 remains the strongest established editable baseline until the owner decides otherwise.

**Comparison:**

- **Archive `159:2`:** the J, blue energy, and surrounding orbit feel like one atmospheric identity. It remains the production fallback, though its small raster source is visibly soft when enlarged.
- **Sonnet v8 `3325:191`:** the textured gold and cyan energy provide richer League-inspired material, but the enclosing rim still makes the J read as a complete badge.
- **Candidate 01 `3482:3`:** improves some counter/material relationships but keeps the enclosing-rim weakness.
- **Candidate 02 `3606:459`:** removes the outer badge and uses a more expressive open cyan flow. That line has more visual signature than Candidate 03, but it reads more graphically than the archive's turbulent energy.
- **Candidate 03 `3619:549`:** the wedge stem and broader negative counter make the J immediately freestanding. At 54px and 32px, the crown and rising hook keep the letter legible; at 16px, the separate ring-free solid glyph is clearer than the complete material mark. Its faceted planes retain the warm-gold/cold-cyan family, while its cleaner geometry trades away atmosphere and ornament.

**Preserve:** archive `159:2` in production; v8 `3325:191` as the established editable baseline; Candidates 01 and 02 as directional evidence; the ring-free small glyphs; compact Foundations `510:14`.

**Owner review question:** does Candidate 03's cleaner, more geometric silhouette make a stronger first impression than Candidate 02's cyan flow while retaining enough of the archive's atmosphere? Compare the large master and native-size proofs; no further J iteration is implied by this review record.
