# Pass79 - archive-led flat macro study

Pass79 is one local, reversible reconstruction traced from the archive target `159:2`. It reduces the mark to a blue orb, one aged-gold orbit stroke, and one filled J silhouette so the comparison stays on crown, upright, hook and J/orbit proportion.

The 468-unit J trace uses an asymmetric shoulder from the broad cap into the upright, a 36-unit vertical shaft, and a rising tapered hook around an open counter. The orbit is an ellipse centered at `(244, 240)` with radii `(177, 178)` behind the J, so the cap and lower bowl cover the rim where their silhouettes cross. No texture, plasma strands, extra rings, Figma nodes, shared assets, or production code were changed.

The comparison board shows the archive, current `1950:6`, Pass16, Pass78 and Pass79 at a common 468px frame, then native 54px / 32px composites and a ring-free 16px reduction beside the separate simplified 16px control.

## Readout

Pass79 preserves the archive's broad upper reach, tall shaft and rising hook in a compact editable shape. It reads as a J at 468px and remains identifiable at 54px and 32px. Its clean single rim still reads more like a conventional badge than the archive's integrated orb, and the flat gold/blue fields lose the archive's authored depth. The archive remains the stronger whole identity and stays the visual standard. Pass79 is not approved or canonical.
