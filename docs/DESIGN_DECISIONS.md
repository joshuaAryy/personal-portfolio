## Controlling decisions

- **League fidelity and assets:** Use the collected Riot, League, and CommunityDragon assets when they are the intended source. Prefer selected authentic references and assets over initials, generic CSS ornaments, homemade shields, generic SVGs, or placeholders. Public release/licensing decisions are separate from active design and implementation; they are not a build gate.
- **Figma-first completion:** Route existence, tests, build success, deployment, HTTP 200, content presence, node count, or a former `IMPLEMENT READY` label do not prove a screen is complete. Completion requires explicit page load, inspection of the active root and relevant archive/reference, rendered implementation, direct comparison, a concrete discrepancy list, meaningful correction, a second render/comparison, and adversarial review with no material visual blocker. Keep unperformed reviews marked pending.
- **Status labels:** Owner feedback overrides old labels. Reopen the canonical J, all six case studies, Help and the error/404 family, Resume Found, and any website surface materially divergent from Figma as `DESIGNING / NEEDS REDESIGN`. Journey keeps its environmental structure but its writing is reopened.
- **First-run flow:** Opening hands off to Home / Explore, then the visitor chooses Projects, Experience, Hackathons, or Education. Archive `510:2` → `69:37` “Home · Mode Selection” is the structural starting point: League-client shell, upper navigation, top-right Profile account entry, right Activity rail and toolbar, horizontal four-mode choice, compact lower-left contextual/refinement queue, and centered Confirm paired with its separate Back disc. Do not substitute a left destination sidebar and single preview panel or a wide two-column detail panel. Profile is not a fifth mode. Refine/filter is optional and cannot block Home.
- **Shared shell and Profile:** The top-right identity/avatar composition is the canonical Profile entry. The large central Joshua lobby card may select/focus first; an explicit second action may open Profile. Use the approved portrait/account composition and selected shell assets.
- **Help and recovery:** Archive `510:2` → `69:207` is the Help interaction source: retain the current client screen underneath a dim blue overlay, highlight relevant controls, and use concise callouts with quick dismissal. On Home, separately target the four modes, lower context queue, and Confirm/Back group. Help is reusable over the current screen, not a standalone guide. Current `2014:11` standalone Help composition is rejected. Unknown, unavailable, empty, and offline conditions are contextual client states, not a generic portfolio guide. Do not invent outages or automatic retry promises.
- **Profile Overview:** Return the website to approved frame `960:2`: banner/background, portrait and identity, CE medallion, Creative / Proactive / Execution, tabs, Projects, lower section signals, connective/overlay logic, right rail, and approved hierarchy. Project identity opens the story; a small source icon may link to a verified public repository. Avoid dashboard cards and large textual action controls.
- **Demos:** Preserve the in-client media browser from `1316:35`, `1316:4534`, and `1298:2`: selector, vertical recording/index treatment, central player, decorative title, and all three entries. Crest playback remains in-client when available; external open is secondary. Cho’Veigo remains an honest static capture. Keep Food Tracker visible in its selector and use the best authentic approved media/mark available; unresolved capture does not remove the entry.
- **Resume:** Keep the exact authorized v13 PDF for embedded native viewer, Download, and Open Fullscreen. Redesign Resume Found with the real Match Found/archive visual language and the J mechanism. Keep `PORTFOLIO UTILITY`, `J`, `RESUME FOUND`, `VIEW RESUME`, and close/home behavior; remove explanatory deck copy.
- **Canonical J:** The active direction is reconstruction/refinement of Archive `510:2` → `147:2` “Logo Pixel Match Lab · v7”, especially its left `159:2` “REFERENCE · Approved AI Target”. Analyze and align that reference against the existing right-side vector reconstructions before editing. Keep first-party vector paths/groups for animation and test at 300, 54, 32, and 16 px. `2280:3474` is discarded greenfield history. - **Case studies:** Rebuild all six around immediate technical proof: problem, role/context/period, system architecture, tools/methods, decisions, evaluation/results, technical ownership, real artifacts, and technical learning. Define common proof requirements, but give each project its own visual grammar. No white/cream figure quota or preserved white-plate formula. Choose dark/light/media/diagram treatment for the technical story; no figure exists just to interrupt scrolling. Preserve source-backed facts and ownership boundaries. Use verified Email/GitHub/LinkedIn/Resume links in a consistent top-right utility area where appropriate.
