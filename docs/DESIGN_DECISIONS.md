# Design Decisions

Public-safe summary of the portfolio's current design direction and the rationale behind it.

| Area | Decision | Rationale and application |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Portfolio shell | Keep a League-client-inspired shell, with original portfolio content and project-specific page compositions. | The shell gives the portfolio a recognizable setting; each story uses its own information hierarchy and evidence instead of copying fictional game data or forcing every project into one detail template. |
| Opening and identity | Use a brief, one-time opening that reveals the J emblem, offers a visible Skip action, and has a reduced-motion path. Keep the emblem legible at small sizes with simplified variants. | The opening gives the site a clear identity without delaying access to the work. The compact mark preserves recognition where the full emblem would lose detail. |
| Project stories | Tell project stories in first person with a concise opening and varied pacing across narrative, diagrams, and authentic media. Use distinct visual grammars rather than repeating a generic report layout. | Visitors can understand the problem, decisions, evidence, and learning without every project reading as the same dashboard or engineering report. |
| Food Tracker | Give the flagship story room for product anatomy, retrieval evaluation, selected iteration moments, engineering boundaries, workflow reflection, and a short close. | The product combines a simple logging experience with careful data and search decisions. Benchmark denominators and catalog-scale figures remain explicit so the evidence cannot be mistaken for user impact. |
| Crest | Center the shared expense-intelligence workflow and make the boundary between retrieved AI context, deterministic rules, and human review visible. End with the supported third-place Brim Financial Challenge result. | This explains how the system supported review while keeping Gemini's role and Joshua's contribution precise. Sample interface data is labeled as such and is not presented as a customer outcome. |
| Cho’Veigo | Center the question of what job fit should mean, then show its evidence-based matching model, human review loop, and system path. Use the selected Recommendations view as static media. | This keeps the story focused on matching quality and avoids suggesting automatic application submission or broad live availability. |
| Fraymakers | Keep the story compact and focus on the thumbnail-generation workflow, composition edge cases, shared project foundation, and unfinished upload prototype. | The page reflects Joshua's defined subsystem contribution and avoids implying that he created the whole tool or completed automated uploads. |
| Experience stories | Keep Living in Silico focused on research learning and distinct experiment contexts. Keep Stush Patties focused on heterogeneous distributor inputs becoming a repeatable reporting handoff. | These are experience narratives rather than consumer-product case studies. Their layouts and evidence should fit their own work, with team contributions and claim limits stated accurately. |
| Journey | Preserve the full-length personal progression story and its five-stop locator. On the website, keep the selected chapter tied to the actual reading position and let the story reflow to fit the viewport. | The locator supports orientation through a long narrative; responsive reading should remain comfortable without shrinking the full story into a fixed poster. |
| Demos | Keep a focused player with a selector for currently available, cleared media. Use small authentic stills to distinguish recordings and label sample data where it appears. | The current website shows only the owner-cleared Crest and Cho’Veigo stills; Food Tracker remains withheld pending authentic media and publication confirmation. The images identify a recording and do not imply product outcomes beyond what it supports. |
| Utilities | Provide a practical Help guide, a clear not-found state, and a recoverable resume flow using the approved general resume. | These routes support visitors who need orientation, route recovery, or access to the resume while staying consistent with the portfolio shell. |
| Motion and accessibility | Keep motion brief and purposeful, provide reduced-motion behavior, and make navigation and recovery controls usable by keyboard. | Motion can clarify transitions and chapter changes; it should not block access or make essential information depend on animation. |
